How Hackathon Team Finder Works

A clear student-to-leader interaction where team creators manually review and approve every incoming squad member.

01

Post Your Problem Statement

Define the target hackathon event, target squad capacity (2 to 6 students), and required tech competencies like React, Python, OpenCV, or Solidity.

02

Pitch Your Background

Browsing students submit a targeted application with their past projects, GitHub links, and department background directly to the team leader.

03

Manual Leader Review & Roster Lock

The team creator reviews all pitches in their dashboard. When an applicant is accepted, they are added to the roster until the team reaches full capacity.
